# Refund Policy

Approved returns are eligible for a refund of the returned product amount.

Refunds are processed after the returned item has been received and inspected.

Refund processing may take several business days after approval.

A refund should not be represented as completed until the system confirms that the refund has been processed.

If a customer disputes a refund amount or claims a refund was not received, the case should be reviewed by support.
